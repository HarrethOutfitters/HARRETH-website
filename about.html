OUR STORY

EVERY GREAT JOURNEY STARTS SOMEWHERE.

HARRETH started with something more meaningful than clothing — family.

Inspired by my children, the name HARRETH represents the people who give this brand its purpose and the desire to build something lasting for the future.

What began as an idea grew into a vision: to create outdoor-inspired apparel and gear for people who work hard, enjoy the outdoors, and aren't afraid to take the road less traveled.

From everyday life to weekend adventures, HARRETH is about carrying that same sense of purpose wherever life takes you.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

BUILT FOR MORE THAN THE MOMENT.

We believe outdoor apparel should be comfortable enough for everyday life and ready to go wherever the next adventure leads.

Our vision is to build a brand that combines rugged outdoor style, practical design, and a commitment to creating something meaningful.

We're building HARRETH one step at a time, with the goal of creating apparel and gear that people can make part of their own journeys.

FAMILY & PURPOSE
